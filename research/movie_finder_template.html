    Source coverageRecords

    These are not complete copies of the entire IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes databases. The IMDb import covers feature film and TV movie records with at least 1,000 votes from its bulk dataset. Rotten Tomatoes title search has per-year result limits, and only titles with checked review counts can qualify through its scores. Missing scores and counts stay unknown; they are never treated as zero or guessed.

    Every listed film requires at least 1,000 IMDb votes. Within that eligible list, IMDb, Tomatometer and Popcornmeter scores qualify films independently. Defaults: IMDb 6.0+ with 1,000+ votes, or Tomatometer 60%+ with 50+ critic reviews. Popcornmeter (60%+ with 1,000+ audience ratings) is off by default; tick it to let audience scores qualify films too. The chosen audience score and its count refer to the same audience group: verified-ticket ratings where shown by RT, otherwise all audience ratings. Raising one score cannot exclude a film that still passes another selected score.

    Badges: Fresh means Tomatometer 60%+. Hot means Popcornmeter 60%+. Certified Fresh and Verified Hot use the designations reported by RT; a high percentage alone does not establish either certification. “Verified Hot” is RT’s official name for the audience certification.

    Romance, horror and documentary categories are excluded by default, including movies whose subgenre contains one of those categories. Indian productions are always left out: JustWatch production country India, or, where JustWatch has no country, Wikidata country of origin India or an IMDb alternative-title pattern typical of Indian releases (only when the recorded language is unknown or Indian). Films with an unknown country otherwise stay in. Only IMDb feature films and TV movies are included; shorts, TV shorts, videos and specials are left out. Ratings that look vote-stuffed are left out too: IMDb 8.5+ from under 10,000 votes with no Rotten Tomatoes score, and films that JustWatch, Rotten Tomatoes and Wikidata know nothing about unless they have 25,000+ IMDb votes. Original language is matched from Wikidata through IMDb identifiers. The default, English, shows films with English among their recorded original languages plus films whose language is unknown; Non-English shows known languages with no English entry. This does not describe which dubbed audio tracks a streamer provides. No genre or language is inferred from a title or production country. The major genre controls collect subgenres in the background; a film can belong to several broad categories. Australian classifications come from the AU catalogue and can differ between editions. Unknown classifications are kept separate; US MPAA ratings are not converted into Australian classifications. Review-count minimums reduce small-sample results; they do not guarantee that a film’s rating will reflect your taste.

    Title matches use an identifier or an unambiguous matching title and release year. Some differently named editions remain separate. Australian streaming offers come from JustWatch and may change. A missing service link means no confirmed matching offer was loaded, rather than proof that a film is unavailable.
