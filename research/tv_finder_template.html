    Coverage, scores & sources

    Source coverageRecords

    Source. IMDb's official non-commercial datasets: title.basics (series, years, genres, runtime), title.ratings (score and votes) and title.episode (which episodes belong to which series). "Last aired" is the latest year any episode was released, using each episode's own year; future announced episodes are ignored. Seasons count the distinct season numbers on IMDb's episode list, so a show with unnumbered episodes may show 0 seasons.

    Who is listed. TV series and miniseries with at least 1,000 IMDb votes, adult titles omitted, that either (1) have IMDb 6.0+ and aired in 2020 or later, by first-air year, end year or an episode year, or (2) first aired in 2000 or later and have IMDb above 8.0. The filters narrow this list; they never add to it.

    Vote-stuffed titles. A small show can reach a near-perfect IMDb score from a few thousand fan votes. By default the finder hides shows rated 9.0 or higher with fewer than 10,000 votes; untick the option under IMDb score to see them. They stay in the file.

    Not covered yet. Australian streaming services, Rotten Tomatoes scores, Australian classifications, production countries and original languages are not collected for TV, so the movie finder's country default (excluding Indian productions) cannot be applied here. "Check Australia" opens a JustWatch search for the title; it is a search, not a confirmed offer. Missing facts stay unknown; they are never guessed.
