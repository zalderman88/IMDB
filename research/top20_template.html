    How the ranking works
    • Each service’s official chart gives 10 points to #1, down to 1 point for #10.
    • A title gets points from every service it charts on, so a hit on two services ranks higher.
    • Netflix also adds a quarter of last week’s points, so shows that stay in the chart count a bit more.
    • Services without an official chart this time (like Stan and Binge) use TMDB’s popularity for titles on that service in Australia, at 60% weight, shown with an outline and “(popular)”.
    • Then a quality bonus of up to 4.5 points: (IMDb rating − 6) × 1.5, for titles with 1,000+ IMDb votes. So a 7.0 adds 1.5 and an 8.5 adds 3.75. Titles under 6.0 get nothing extra, but aren’t removed.
    • Indian productions are left out, as on the other pages.
